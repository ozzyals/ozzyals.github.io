C++ Console Application

Fall Shell Asylum

Fall Shell Asylum is a text-based survival horror game where the player explores a grid-based asylum, managing their Sanity as they uncover items, lore, and the building's dark history. Movement is menu-driven, with a minimap that reveals areas as they're explored. Depending on what the player finds and does, the game ends in one of three ways — succumbing to the asylum, escaping without answers, or uncovering the full truth before getting out.

Key Features

  • Grid-based exploration with a revealing minimap — the player navigates a 5x5 asylum using WASD movement, with a minimap that only shows rooms once they've been explored or are adjacent to explored territory.
  • Branching win/loss conditions — the game tracks the player's Sanity and inventory to produce one of three distinct endings, from succumbing to the asylum to fully uncovering its hidden history.
